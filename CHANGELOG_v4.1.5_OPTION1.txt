Sri Nidhi Study Hall v4.1.5 — OPTION 1

Public Admission Payment Flow
- Fixed UPI ID: 9390856544@ybl
- Exact admission amount is pre-filled in PhonePe / any UPI app.
- Public applicant must enter UTR / Transaction ID.
- No Admission Number, Student ID, Password or Admission PDF is generated before admin verification.
- Admin Admissions page shows pending UPI payment requests.
- Admin checks UTR and exact amount in PhonePe / bank statement, then selects Verify Payment & Approve.
- Only after verification are Admission Number, Student ID, Password, fee receipt and Admission Confirmation PDF generated.
- Manual Admission remains available to the admin.

Important
A normal UPI deep link cannot independently confirm the bank payment. Admin verification is mandatory in this Option 1 release.
